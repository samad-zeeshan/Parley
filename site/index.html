Parley

Parley answers the phone for a property agency, in English, Gulf Arabic or both in one sentence. It books a viewing, only says prices and addresses that came from the agency's own listings, and works with no internet connection. Pick a call and press play.

    Fact check

    Parley checks every fact first

    Before Parley speaks, it checks each fact in its reply, such as a date, a price or a phone number. Each fact must match what the booking system told it during this call. If a fact does not match, Parley does not use that reply.
